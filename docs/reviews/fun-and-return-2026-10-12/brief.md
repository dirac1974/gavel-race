## What David is deciding here (his direction, not the team's call)
These reverse earlier provisional team decisions, so the design has to deliver them in a kid-safe form. It must not argue them away.

1. **Over-running can need the vet.**
   - This is David's second ask: 2026-10-07 (OPEN_QUESTIONS 5) and again now.
   - D-058 chose Spa-only, with no injury. David now says "We don't want sick, but over running horses could cause injury or care at the vet needed."
   - The team designs the gentlest version that is still real:
     - it comes from over-running, never from neglect;
     - no permanent harm, no death, no illness;
     - no paid cure, no Diamonds;
     - clear, kind copy that never blames the kid;
     - the vet visit is a positive play moment;
     - the cause is visible and avoidable.
   - Hard rule 3 still binds: never die, sicken or run away from neglect; no streak resets; no fake urgency.
2. **Horses age and become less effective, and can be sold.**
   - This reverses D-059's "No ageing, no decline".
   - Design an arc: youngster → prime → veteran → retirement (Legend paddock, breeding, rehoming or selling).
   - The decline is gentle, visible ahead of time and never a punishment, and it gives a reason to raise the next horse.
   - Rehoming already exists (D-059, 50% back). Decide whether "sold" stays NPC-only. There's still no player trading: Roblox policy and scam risk.
3. **Visit other users' stables, not only friends'.**
   - D-060 is friends only.
   - Design safe visiting for anyone in the server: owner privacy setting, no chat surfaces beyond Roblox's own, no griefing.
4. **The queue** must be explained clearly in the UI ("how does the queue work for each one?"). David couldn't tell how it works.
5. **Horse classes to qualify for certain races and prestige.**
   - Today classes are labels only (D-062: First Win / Rising Star / Open, plus the league Cup).
   - Design a real qualification ladder: for example Maiden → Allowance → Stakes → Graded, by horse record, opening prestige races and titles.
