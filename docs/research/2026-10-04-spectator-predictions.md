# Spectator predictions and Roblox gambling policy (2026-10-04)

## Question
Can spectators back a horse for a reward, and what makes that allowed or not?

## Answer
- Being unbuyable is no longer the safe line. Roblox banned simulated gambling on 2023-06-20 (enforced from 2023-09-18), including play with "virtual chips", whether or not the currency can be bought. Before that, gambling with non-Robux currency was allowed.
- What reviewers look for: something staked on a chance outcome (even free currency), a reward that is an item of value, and betting-shaped presentation (odds, order picks, rewards that grow with how unlikely the pick was).
- A free pick with a flat, non-tradeable cosmetic reward clears the written rules. Scaling the reward by long shot is betting odds without the stake: no clear written ban, but a real moderation and maturity-label risk.

## Evidence
- [Roblox Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards) (checked 2026-10-04): unplayable gambling content allowed; "both simulated and actual gambling activities" prohibited; contests with Robux or real-money prizes need Roblox's direct involvement.
- [Content Maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity) (checked 2026-10-04): gambling is "exchanging real world money, Robux, or in-experience items of value for a game of chance"; only unplayable gambling content may be depicted. Roblox Kids (5–8) and Select (9–15) take only Minimal or Mild experiences.
- [DevForum: Is gambling allowed?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-gambling-allowed/1561098): the 2023 announcement and deadline; the ban applies whether or not currency is buyable.
- [DevForum: "Unplayable Gambling Content"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarification-on-unplayable-gambling-content-in-roblox-community-standards/4167854) (2025-12): community view that "unplayable" means props only; reports of reward-free blackjack games being removed. Not staff.

## Implications
- Decided as D-019: cheer for a rider, earn cosmetic Fan XP from that rider's taps, flat small win bonus, no cost, never scaled by win chance.
- The lobby's win chance plus purse (purse = B / q) carries the same information as odds. Defensible for riders (nothing staked, own horse), but keep pick buttons away from it and consider an "Upset bonus" label or prize tiers instead of exact numbers (debate candidate).

## Uncertainties
- No Roblox staff ruling on free prediction games was found; forum answers are from community members.
- Whether depicting anything gambling-like changes the maturity label wasn't confirmed. Re-check before launch and answer the Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire conservatively.
