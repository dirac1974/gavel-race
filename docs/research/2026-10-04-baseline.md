# Baseline research (planning session, 2026-10-04)

## Roblox gambling policy
- **Answer:** simulated and actual gambling are both prohibited; free, unbuyable currency does not make betting allowed.
- **Evidence:** [Roblox Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards) (checked 2026-10-04): unplayable gambling imagery allowed; simulated and actual gambling prohibited; no real money, Robux, or in-game items of value exchanged in connection with gambling. Simulated gambling ban took effect 2023-09-18 ([DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-gambling-allowed/1561098)).
- **Paid random items:** odds must be disclosed before purchase, every outcome must give value, `PolicyService.ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` must gate access; paid item trading needs `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` (same page).

## Implications
- No current horse game pairs a real skill race with a deep stable; that is the gap.
- Weekly updates, offline progress, collection, and care-driven attachment are the proven retention drivers.

## Uncertainties
- Player-count figures are third-party estimates.
- Policy can change; re-check before launch.
